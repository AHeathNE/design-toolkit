Contact Sheet

Eleven small tools for images, motion, print, file naming, and sound — each one a single self-contained page that runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you open here is ever uploaded anywhere.

Image editing

3 tools
IMG 01

Darkroom

Crop and resize an image, then push it through an effect — adjust and cutout, duotone, posterize, a pixel-shift glitch, or a straight bitmap export.

Any image in
Open Darkroom
IMG 02

Squeeze

A JPEG compressor for one image or a whole batch, with adjustable quality and a max width or height. Download everything back as a zip.

Any image in → compressed JPG out
Open Squeeze
IMG 03

Format Swap

Converts images between PNG, JPG, WEBP, GIF, and BMP using the browser's own canvas. JPG output has no transparency — clear areas fill white.

PNG · JPG · WEBP · GIF · BMP
Open Format Swap

Motion & GIF

3 tools
MOT 01

Frame Extractor

Pull a single still frame out of a video or GIF, or trim a clip down — optionally at a lower frame rate — and export it as a GIF or video.

Video works best in Chrome or Edge
Open Frame Extractor
MOT 02

GIF Editor

Load an animated GIF to crop it and adjust brightness, contrast, hue, and saturation, invert colors, posterize, then export the result.

Animated GIF in → animated GIF out
Open GIF Editor
MOT 03

GIF Combiner

Combine two or more GIFs or images into one, either playing in sequence or arranged in a grid, with a matched output size and frame timing.

GIFs & images → one GIF
Open GIF Combiner

Print & layout

3 tools
PRT 01

Layout Studio

Arrange, crop, and export image layouts onto a page you define by size, DPI, and margins — drag, resize, or use an auto-arrange preset.

Sets its own page size & DPI
Open Layout Studio
PRT 02

Print Shop

Assembles a PDF from your pages — a quick merge or a structured bind — then imposes it into signatures ready for printing.

PDF pages in → imposed PDF out
Open Print Shop
PRT 03

Tile Studio

Slices an image into a grid, then lets you swap tiles in place or scatter them freely, with control over seam gap and tile tilt.

One image → a rearranged grid
Open Tile Studio

Files & naming

1 tool
FIL 01

Claim & Draw

Drop in a pile of files, give them a base name, and shuffle them into a random order — each one comes back renamed with a padded claim number.

Any file type → renamed copies, zipped
Open Claim & Draw

Sound

1 tool
SND 01

Audio Studio

A client-side cutter and MP3 encoder. Mark cut points on the waveform, trim what you don't need, and compress the rest.

MP3 · WAV · M4A · OGG · FLAC in → MP3 out
Open Audio Studio
Every tool on this sheet processes files locally in your browser — opening one, editing, and exporting never sends anything over the network.
